`lines.json` holds the side-check lines that `tools/render_lines.py` draws. They come from two sources:

- **Lines marked `"source": "tawiki"`** are pieces of sentences from the opening sections of Tamil Wikipedia
  articles, written by Wikipedia's contributors and shared under the
  [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
  Lines were cut at spaces into pieces of 25 to 55 characters; nothing else was changed.
  You may share and adapt them under the same licence, with credit to the articles below.
- **Lines marked `"source": "own"`** were written for this project (numbers, dates, signs and the characters
  being checked). They are under the repository's Apache License 2.0.

None of these lines is ever used to train the Tamil OCR models; they are only for testing.
